## 1. Research Title
Machine Learning-Driven Optimized Land Use Mix Planning for Sustainable
Urban Development

## 2. Journal Target
Sustainability Analytics and Modeling (Elsevier/IFORS)
ISSN: 2667-2596 | Open Access | APC covered by IFORS

## 3. Problem Formulation (Refined)
### Objective
Develop a data-driven framework to optimize vehicle fleet composition for
sustainable urban transportation systems, maximizing environmental
performance (fuel efficiency, low emissions, green vehicle adoption)
while maintaining vehicle functionality and economic viability.

### Input Variables
- Vehicle characteristics: make, model, body type, vehicle class, segment
- Engine specifications: displacement, cylinders, power, torque
- Fuel systems: fuel type (petrol, diesel, electric, hybrid), injection type
- Efficiency metrics: fuel consumption (city/highway/combined L/100km),
  fuel efficiency score, energy consumption (EV)
- Emissions: CO2 emissions (g/km), emission standard
- Powertrain: transmission type, drivetrain, EV/hybrid flags
- Usage context: annual driving KM, commute distance, driver profile
- Economic factors: pricing segments, financing options, monthly payment

### Target Variables
- Environmental Score (sustainability index, 0-100, higher=better)
- Green Vehicle Score (sustainability-oriented vehicle attribute)
- Fuel Efficiency Score (efficiency-oriented vehicle attribute)
- CO2 Emissions (g/km) as proxy for environmental impact

### Methodology
1. Statistical learning: Multiple Linear Regression, Ridge, Lasso, SVR
2. Tree-based: Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost
3. Evaluation: RMSE, MAE, R2, feature importance, permutation importance
4. Optimization: Multi-objective optimization for optimal fleet composition
5. Validation: 3-seed cross-validation (R2: CV1, CV2, CV3)

### Journal Fit
- Methods: Multiple regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, multi-objective optimization
- Scope: Sustainable Cities and Communities (SDG 11), Climate Action (SDG 13)
- Real-world data: Kaggle Cars Dataset (CC0-1.0, 100K rows, 437 features)
- Interdisciplinary: Data science + transportation + sustainability
